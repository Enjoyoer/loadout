Use these as preferred source classes:

* Official Linux/BSD project download pages and torrents.
* Official software/vendor downloads.
* Internet Archive items with download rights.
* Public Domain Torrents or other explicit public-domain catalogs.
* Creator/distributor pages for Creative Commons releases.
* User-provided direct URLs/magnets.

Avoid:

* Unofficial torrents for commercial movies, TV, music, books, games, or software.
* Tracker links with private passkeys in chat or docs.
