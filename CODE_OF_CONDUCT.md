# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not accepted.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer ([@Enjoyoer](https://github.com/Enjoyoer)) through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability") and mark the report as a conduct issue. Reports are reviewed within 7 days and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block participants who break these rules.
